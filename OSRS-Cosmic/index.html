PvM & Raid Programs

We teach beginner bossing and field high-efficiency raid teams.

Chambers of Xeric (CoX)

Whether you're looking for chill 3+1s, high-scale masses, or Challenge Mode (CM) speeds, our members run CoX daily.

  • Recommended Gear: Full Elite Void / Bandos / Bowfa / Tridents
  • Frequency: Multiple raids hosted daily
  • Learners: Scouting and callout instruction provided

Minimum Stats Recommended

  • 80+ Attack, Strength, Defense
  • 85+ Ranged & Magic
  • 74+ Prayer (Rigour / Augury unlocked preferred)

Tombs of Amascut (ToA)

From entry-mode learning runs up to 400+ invocation Expert runs, ToA is a core staple of our daily PvM activity.

  • Recommended Gear: Fang, Blowpipe, Ahrim's / Ancestral
  • Invocations: Tailored to the group's experience level
  • Purples: Split or Free-For-All (FFA) teams clearly marked

Requirements

  • Quest: Beneath Cursed Sands
  • 75+ Combat Stats for Entry / 90+ for Experts

Theatre of Blood (ToB)

Step into the Vampyric arena with experienced leads who teach roles, tick-eating, and boss mechanics safely.

  • Recommended Gear: Scythe / Blade of Saeldor, Chally, Blowpipe
  • Focus: Role assignment (Melee, Range, Freeze, Mage)

Requirements

  • 90+ Melee Stats, 90+ Ranged & Magic
  • Discord Voice Chat capability (Mute is fine)

Clan Mentor System

Never done a raid before? Don't worry. Cosmic Order has dedicated PvM Mentors to get you your first KC safely.

  • Gear check & inventory setup assistance
  • Tile indicators and plugin configuration guides
  • Patient, zero-flame environment

How to Request a Mentor

Join our Discord server and post in the #pvm-mentorship channel with your current stats and equipment!
